# Documentation Update Summary

This document summarizes the documentation changes made to reflect updates to the Lambda throttling remediation logic.

## Code Changes
- **Safety buffer multiplier**: Raised from `1.10` to `1.25` (commit a21d25d)
- **Minimum floor**: Raised from `5` to `10` (commit a21d25d)
- **Rationale**: Stronger safety margin for traffic variance and more conservative baseline protection

## Files Updated

### 1. `docs/lambda-throttling.md`
- **Example output**: Updated example calculation from `44 (max(5, ceil(40 * 1.10)))` to `50 (max(10, ceil(40 * 1.25)))`
- **Section: Why a 1.25 safety buffer?** (formerly "Why a 1.10 safety buffer?"): 
  - Clarified that bursts to ~1.25x average are common and that the 1.25x multiplier provides a stronger safety margin
  - Repositioned rationale to align with new multiplier value
- **Section: Why a floor of 10?** (formerly "Why a floor of 5?"): 
  - Updated to reflect the conservative floor of 10 instead of 5
  - Explained that the higher floor is conservative enough to handle most traffic variance scenarios

### 2. `docs/remediation-playbooks.md`
- **Recommendation logic section**: 
  - Updated minimum floor from `5` to `10`
  - Updated safety multiplier from `1.10` to `1.25`
  - Preserved the contract explanation that these rules ensure the agent never recommends values below current production load

## Impact
These documentation updates ensure that operators understand the more conservative recommendations generated by the updated remediation logic. The higher safety buffer (1.25x) and higher floor (10) provide better protection against traffic spikes and throttling incidents in production environments.
